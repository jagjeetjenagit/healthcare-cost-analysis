Key Findings
🌎
Regional Cost Disparity
West region costs run ~30% above the national average ($30.8K vs $22.3K), while Midwest is the most cost-efficient region.
🏥
Oncology Drives Highest Cost
Oncology has the highest per-patient avg cost at $67.9K — nearly 3x the overall average. Cardiovascular and Orthopedic follow.
🏨
Inpatient Cost Concentration
Inpatient admissions drive ~65% of total costs despite being less than 40% of all cases — a major cost concentration risk.
💳
Self-Pay Financial Burden
Self-pay patients bear 1.4x the cost burden of insured patients, indicating significant financial toxicity risk for uninsured individuals.
👴
Age-Cost Correlation
Patients aged 61+ incur 1.8x the avg cost of patients under 30, highlighting the financial impact of an aging patient population.
📅
Q4 Cost Spike
Consistent Q4 cost and utilization spikes across all years — likely driven by elective procedures before year-end insurance resets.
