Analysis Report

Executive Summary

Analysis of 500 patient records across 5 US regions (2022–2024) reveals significant cost disparities driven by geography, diagnosis type, treatment setting, and insurance status. The West region and Oncology diagnosis category are the primary cost drivers, while inpatient admissions concentrate the majority of expenditure despite representing a minority of cases. Self-pay patients face disproportionate financial burden, and Q4 shows consistent utilisation spikes — patterns that have direct implications for healthcare cost management strategy.

SQL Highlights

Regional Cost vs National Average
WITH national AS (
  SELECT AVG(Treatment_Cost) avg_cost
  FROM healthcare_patients
)
SELECT
  Region,
  AVG(Treatment_Cost)        region_avg,
  ROUND(
    (AVG(Treatment_Cost) - n.avg_cost)
    / n.avg_cost * 100, 1
  )                           pct_vs_national
FROM healthcare_patients, national n
GROUP BY Region, n.avg_cost
ORDER BY region_avg DESC;
Top 10% High-Cost Patients
WITH ranked AS (
  SELECT
    Patient_ID, Diagnosis,
    Treatment_Cost,
    PERCENTILE_CONT(0.90)
      WITHIN GROUP
      (ORDER BY Treatment_Cost)
      OVER ()    p90_threshold
  FROM healthcare_patients
)
SELECT Patient_ID, Diagnosis,
  Treatment_Cost
FROM ranked
WHERE Treatment_Cost >= p90_threshold
ORDER BY Treatment_Cost DESC;
