What This Dashboard Tells Us
Key insights derived from the analysis of 500 patient records across 5 regions — 2022 to 2024.
Regional Cost
+33%
West Region is Most Expensive
West region averages $29,780/patient — 33% above the national average of $22,331. Midwest is the most cost-efficient at $16,550.
Diagnosis Cost
$58.6K
Oncology Drives the Highest Cost
Oncology patients cost $58,609 on average — 2.6× the overall mean. Cardiovascular ($29.5K) and Orthopedic ($27.3K) are next highest.
Treatment Mix
54%
Inpatient Concentrates Most Spend
Inpatient cases are only 37% of all admissions yet drive ~54% of total expenditure at $32,738 avg — the single biggest cost lever.
Insurance Gap
28%
Private Insurance Sees Highest Costs
Private insurance patients average $23,614 vs Self-Pay at $18,441 — a 28% gap. Lower self-pay costs may reflect reduced care access for the 62 uninsured patients in the dataset.
Regional Gap
1.8×
West Costs 1.8× More Than Midwest
West region ($29,780) costs 1.8× more than the cheapest region, Midwest ($16,550) — the largest regional cost gap in the dataset, representing a $13,230 spread per patient.
Seasonal Trend
Q3/Q4
Mid-to-Year-End Cost Spikes
Q3 2022 ($28,136) and Q4 2023 ($28,166) are the two highest-cost quarters — 26–27% above their annual averages, likely driven by elective procedure cycles.
HEATMAP INSIGHT
West + Oncology = Highest Risk Combination
The Region × Diagnosis heatmap reveals the West–Oncology intersection at $70,130 — 3.1× the national average of $22,331. This is the single most expensive patient segment and requires priority cost management focus.
